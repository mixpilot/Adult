# Profile Filling Guide

This guide helps you fill out your escort profile form with appropriate information.

---

## 📝 **Field-by-Field Guide**

### **1. Gender**
**What to select:**
- Choose the option that best describes you:
  - `Female` - Most common for escort profiles
  - `Male`
  - `Non-Binary`
  - `Other`
  - `Prefer not to say`

**Example:** Select `Female`

---

### **2. Seeking**
**What to select:**
- This indicates who you're looking for or who you provide services to:
  - `Everyone` - Open to all clients (most common for escorts)
  - `Male` - Only male clients
  - `Female` - Only female clients
  - `Non-Binary` - Only non-binary clients

**Example:** Select `Everyone` (if you're open to all clients)

**Tip:** Most escorts choose "Everyone" to maximize their client base.

---

### **3. Age**
**What to enter:**
- Enter your actual age (must be 18 or older)
- This is a number between 18 and 100

**Example:** `25`, `28`, `32`, etc.

**Tip:** Be honest - clients appreciate authenticity. Age verification may be required.

---

### **4. Location**
**What to enter:**
- Enter your general location/area
- This is a text field, so you can be specific

**Examples:**
- `Kasarani`
- `Westlands, Nairobi`
- `Parklands`
- `Kilimani`

**Tip:** Be specific enough for clients to find you, but not too detailed for privacy.

---

### **5. More detailed description, building or street (optional)**
**What to enter:**
- This is optional - only fill if you want to be more specific
- Can include building name, street, or landmark

**Examples:**
- `Building 123, Parklands Road`
- `Near Junction Mall`
- `Thika Road, Kimbo`
- Leave empty if you prefer privacy

**Tip:** Many escorts leave this empty for privacy and share exact location only after connecting with verified clients.

---

### **6. Bio**
**What to write:**
- This is your main profile description (max 500 characters)
- Write about yourself, your services, personality, and what makes you special
- Be professional, engaging, and authentic

**Good Examples:**

**Example 1 (Professional & Elegant):**
```
Elegant and sophisticated companion offering discreet, professional services. 
I specialize in dinner dates, social events, and private companionship. 
Fluent in English and Swahili. Non-smoker, social drinker. 
Available for both in-call and out-call services. 
Discretion and professionalism guaranteed.
```

**Example 2 (Friendly & Approachable):**
```
Friendly and fun-loving companion looking to make your day special. 
I enjoy good conversation, fine dining, and creating memorable experiences. 
Available for dates, events, and private meetings. 
Let's connect and see where the evening takes us!
```

**Example 3 (Detailed & Specific):**
```
Professional escort with 5+ years experience. I offer:
- Dinner dates and social events
- Travel companionship
- Private meetings (in-call/out-call)
- GFE (Girlfriend Experience)

I'm well-educated, well-traveled, and enjoy art, music, and fine dining. 
Discreet, professional, and reliable. Available 24/7 for verified clients.
```

**Tips for Writing Bio:**
- ✅ Be authentic and honest
- ✅ Highlight your unique qualities
- ✅ Mention your availability (in-call, out-call, travel)
- ✅ Include languages you speak
- ✅ Mention if you're non-smoker/smoker, drinker/non-drinker
- ✅ Keep it professional but warm
- ✅ Avoid explicit language (platform rules)
- ❌ Don't make false claims
- ❌ Don't use explicit descriptions

---

### **7. Interests**
**What to enter:**
- Enter your hobbies and interests (comma-separated)
- This helps clients find common ground with you
- Be specific but not too long

**Good Examples:**

**Example 1:**
```
Travel, Fine Dining, Art, Music, Reading, Fitness, Yoga
```

**Example 2:**
```
Movies, Concerts, Dancing, Wine Tasting, Photography, Fashion
```

**Example 3:**
```
Cooking, Travel, Literature, Jazz Music, Beach Activities, Shopping
```

**Common Interest Categories:**
- **Entertainment:** Movies, Music, Concerts, Theater, Comedy Shows
- **Food & Drink:** Fine Dining, Wine Tasting, Cooking, Coffee
- **Travel:** Travel, Adventure, Beach, Mountains, City Exploration
- **Sports & Fitness:** Fitness, Yoga, Swimming, Running, Gym
- **Arts & Culture:** Art, Literature, Museums, Photography, Fashion
- **Social:** Dancing, Nightlife, Social Events, Networking
- **Relaxation:** Reading, Spa, Meditation, Nature Walks

**Tips:**
- ✅ List 5-10 interests
- ✅ Mix different categories
- ✅ Be genuine (clients may ask about them)
- ✅ Use commas to separate
- ❌ Don't list too many (looks fake)
- ❌ Don't use interests you don't actually have

## ✅ **Profile Completeness Tips**

**Minimum for Visibility:**
- Gender ✓
- Seeking ✓
- Age ✓
- Location ✓
- Bio (at least 50 characters) ✓
- At least one profile photo (upload separately)
- Phone verification (done separately)

**For Best Results:**
- Fill all fields completely
- Write a detailed, engaging bio (200-500 characters)
- List 5-10 genuine interests
- Upload multiple high-quality photos
- Complete phone verification

---

## 📸 **About Photos**

**Photo Requirements:**
- Upload at least 1 photo to be visible in search
- More photos = more profile views
- Use clear, high-quality images
- Show your face (builds trust)
- Mix of full-body and close-up photos works best

**Photo Tips:**
- ✅ Use good lighting
- ✅ Dress appropriately
- ✅ Smile and look approachable
- ✅ Use recent photos
- ❌ Don't use heavily filtered photos
- ❌ Don't use photos that don't look like you

---

## 🔒 **Privacy Considerations**

**What to Share:**
- ✅ General area (e.g., "Westlands")
- ✅ Age range
- ✅ Interests and personality
- ✅ Services offered (in general terms)

**What NOT to Share:**
- ❌ Exact address (use "More detailed description" only after connecting)
- ❌ Real full name
- ❌ Personal phone number (use platform messaging)
- ❌ Financial information

---

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

1. **Be Authentic:** Clients appreciate honesty and authenticity
2. **Update Regularly:** Keep your bio and interests current
3. **Professional Tone:** Maintain a professional but warm tone
4. **Highlight Uniqueness:** What makes you different from others?
5. **Call to Action:** End bio with something like "Let's connect!" or "Available for verified clients"
6. **Proofread:** Check for spelling and grammar errors
7. **Be Specific:** "Fine dining" is better than just "food"

---

## ❓ **Common Questions**

**Q: Should I be very detailed in my bio?**
A: Yes, but keep it professional. 200-400 characters is ideal.

**Q: Can I change my profile later?**
A: Yes! You can update your profile anytime.

**Q: What if I don't want to share my exact location?**
A: That's fine! Just use a general area like "Westlands" and leave the detailed description empty.

**Q: How many interests should I list?**
A: 5-10 is perfect. Too few looks incomplete, too many looks fake.

**Q: Should I mention rates in my bio?**
A: No, discuss rates privately with clients after connecting.
